About OVEGO LLC

Built for long-term partnerships, not one-off orders

OVEGO LLC is a U.S.-registered wholesale ecommerce company focused on building long-term relationships with manufacturers and authorized distributors.

Partnership process

From first contact to long-term cooperation

A straightforward six-step process, the same for every brand we work with.

1

Initial Contact

Contact our purchasing team and provide basic information about your company and product range.

2

Wholesale Information

Share your wholesale catalog, pricing, minimum-order requirements, reseller policy, and MAP policy where applicable.

3

Account Review

We review the product range, purchasing requirements, documentation, and partnership terms.

4

Account Approval

Once approved, we complete the wholesale account setup and confirm purchasing conditions.

5

First Purchase Order

We place the initial order based on product demand and availability — 100% prepaid, with terms signed before a single unit ships.

6

Long-Term Cooperation

We continue purchasing based on performance, inventory needs, and the development of the partnership.

FAQ

Common questions from brands

Is OVEGO LLC a wholesaler?

Yes. OVEGO LLC purchases products from manufacturers and authorized distributors for resale through established ecommerce channels.

Do you work directly with brands?

Yes. We welcome direct partnerships with manufacturers and authorized distributors.

Where do you sell?

We distribute products through established ecommerce marketplaces and online retail channels. Specific sales channels can be discussed during the wholesale account-review process.

Do you comply with MAP policies?

Yes. We respect MAP and other agreed pricing policies where applicable.

Do you sell authentic products only?

Yes. We purchase authentic products through documented wholesale sources.

Do you use your own warehouses?

We work with third-party prep and fulfillment partners in the United States. These facilities support receiving, inspection, preparation, storage, fulfillment, and outbound shipping.

Do you purchase by case or pallet?

Order format depends on the product category, demand, minimum-order requirements, and supplier terms.

How do you pay?

Approved orders are paid 100% upfront before shipment — no consignment. Terms for ongoing orders are agreed individually as the partnership develops.

What product categories do you purchase?

We currently purchase products in health and wellness, beauty and personal care, household, grocery, pet, and baby categories.

What documents can you provide?

Business registration and resale documentation are provided during the supplier account-opening process.

How can I become a supplier?

Complete the supplier application below or contact our purchasing team directly.
